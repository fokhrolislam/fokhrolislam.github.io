Sabaq (ছবক)

Madrasa and school management for Bangladesh. Most of its users had never used software before: teachers, office staff and parents on cheap Android phones and slow connections.

  • Multi-tenant Laravel SaaS: every institution has its own site and database, 370+ of them updated from one script with contract gates and per-site rollback.
  • Flutter app on the Play Store for staff and guardians. Fees, attendance, results, prints and push notifications, with writes that wait for the network when it drops.
  • A Sanctum API that mirrors the web app field for field, so the phone and the office screen always show the same numbers.
  • Support tools for the team: a Gemini helpline bot, an answer finder that understands misspelt Bangla, and a WhatsApp ad-lead tracker.
